## Platform

web

## Users

Anyone on the public internet who presents to a live or remote audience, treated as equally important: trainers and workshop facilitators (cohorts, client sessions), teachers and educators (classroom, self-paced courses), and business presenters (client pitches, internal meetings, town halls). Presenters build and run decks on a laptop; audience members join on their phones with a code or QR link, usually with no account.

## Product Purpose

Densight Slides is a platform for building and presenting interactive slide decks with live audience polls, quizzes and engagement blocks. A deck is plain JSON drawn by one renderer for the editor, presenter and exports. Success: a presenter makes a good-looking interactive deck quickly, runs it live or as a self-paced course, and the audience participates without friction. Built by Numan Ahmad / Densight Labs, first for his own workshops and cohorts, now as a public product (live at densight-slides.netlify.app).

## Positioning

A free-form design canvas where polls, quizzes, branching and other interaction are blocks inside the deck itself, not a separate poll tool bolted to slides. Backed by: works offline and with no account (decks live on-device, sign-in only for sync and sharing), and a privacy-first stance (private "keep nothing" sessions, plain-language legal pages, no AI features).

## Operating Context

- Presenter: dashboard (`/app/`), editor (`/app/d/<id>`), presenter view with laser, drawing, spotlight and overview, fullscreen, phone remote.
- Audience: phone pages via `?join=CODE`, shared read-only viewer via `?view=TOKEN`.
- Public pages (`/`, `/learn/`, `/privacy/`, `/terms/`, pricing) are static HTML generated by `tools/site.mjs`.
- Exports: standalone HTML, PDF, PNG, PPTX. Imports: .pptx.
- Hosting on Netlify; Supabase for auth, cloud decks, realtime broadcast, teams. Netlify credits are limited; changes are batched and gated by `npm run qa`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Free-form canvas editor (frame, lock, hide, group, snap, animation) plus legacy fixed layouts; themes and theme builder; light and dark.
- Interactive blocks: button/branching, quiz, tabs, accordion, hotspots, flip cards, reveal, before/after, poll, word cloud, Q&A, ranking, reactions, pin-on-image, tables, 2x2 matrix, shapes, icons, diagrams.
- Live sessions: timed quizzes, names/teams/leaderboard, moderation and swear filter, session reports, self-paced courses with completions and certificates, private sessions.
- Plans (confirmed as built): Free, Starter, Pro (5 team seats), Advanced (25 team seats). Word clouds, open answers, Q&A, moderation, timed quizzes and reports, and self-paced courses are Starter and above.
- Team workspaces with comments and shared edit access (no real-time co-editing).
- English only. Vanilla JS, no framework.
- Excluded unless Numan says otherwise: AI features, PPTX-import expansion, real-time co-editing. Honest "Not yet" on the home page: PowerPoint/Google Slides add-ins, SSO, real-time co-editing.
- Not verified in the real world: Safari/Firefox, real iPhones, real email, Google sign-in, team flows against a real Supabase project.
- Never `git push` or deploy; commit locally and ask Numan to push.

## Brand Commitments

Densight Labs identity: logo mark in `site/brand/` (dark and light variants), `#2020E8` blue, `#0F1117` ink, `#F8F9FB` surface, Inter for interface, Newsreader serif for public-page headlines, 16-24px radii. Tokens live in `src/styles/tokens.css`. Public pages are deliberately plain: hairline rules and plain text rather than pill badges, chips or card grids; flat surfaces; no decorative motion (a test enforces this). Voice: plain language, no emojis. Support contact `hello@densight.com`.

## Evidence on Hand

Real product with 388+ tests and a QA gate. No customer logos, testimonials or usage numbers exist; do not fabricate any. Legal pages are plain-language drafts pending Numan's review.

## Product Principles

1. The deck is the product: interaction lives inside slides, not beside them.
2. Zero friction for the audience: join by code or QR, no account, works on any phone.
3. Honest about limits: say what is not built yet; never claim unverified compatibility.
4. Local-first and private by default; sign-in and cloud are opt-in.
5. Powerful for presenters, plain and fast for everyone else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Pages are axe-clean in light and dark, and palette contrast pairs are tested. Audience pages must work on small phones and slow connections. An accessibility checker for decks and RTL language support were deliberately excluded for now.
